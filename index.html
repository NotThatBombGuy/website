Deltarune

Deltarune is a RPG developed by Toby Fox... yeah I don't have to say much you probably already know about it. I've grown up with Toby's games, starting with Undertale when I was just a wee little lad. His way of developing stories is simply amazing, and don't get me started on the music. If you haven't played this, GO PLAY IT NOW.

Eternal Towers of Hell

EToH (Short for Eternal Towers of Hell) is a Tower-Styled obby game where you complete obbies of various difficulties without checkpoints. I've been playing this for years, and even though it can be miserable to play sometimes, it has always stuck with me.

Subnautica

Subnautica was the game that REALLY got me into gaming. It still holds up to today's standards, and it still stands as my favorite game out of the trilogy. The design of the creatures and the environments are just top notch. Definitely a must-play.
